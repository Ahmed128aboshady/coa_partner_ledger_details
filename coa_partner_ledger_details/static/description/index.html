No More Navigating Away from the Partner Ledger

Standard Odoo Enterprise Partner Ledger only shows invoice totals and reference numbers. Confirming what products were billed requires opening dozens of browser tabs.

Without COA Partner Ledger Invoice Drill-down

  • Auditors must open each invoice in a new browser tab to check product line contents.
  • Debt collection calls stall while accountants search for item details requested by clients.
  • No inline visibility into invoiced quantities or units of measure from the ledger.
  • Massive productivity loss during month-end closes and external financial audits.

With COA Partner Ledger Invoice Drill-down

  • Interactive expand arrow next to every invoice in the Partner Ledger.
  • Instantly unfold product rows: Product Name, Invoiced Qty, UoM, and Line Total.
  • Review customer and vendor balances without ever leaving the reporting screen.
  • Built using official Odoo report framework: zero broken line IDs and zero database alterations.

Engineered for Enterprise Financial Reporting

Clean extension of Odoo Enterprise dynamic report handler for high-performance drill-downs.

Expandable Invoice Rows

Adds an interactive toggle next to every customer invoice and vendor bill row inside the dynamic Partner Ledger report.

Product Description & Name

Displays the full product name, catalog reference, and invoice line description clearly indented under the parent move.

Quantity & Unit of Measure

Renders invoiced quantities alongside their standard Units of Measure (Units, Kg, Hours) directly in the report label.

Line Total in Balance Column

Renders the exact product line subtotal aligned in the report's native Balance column without disturbing debit/credit parity.

Official Framework Compliance

Uses Odoo's official account.report._get_generic_line_id() helper to guarantee stability across minor version updates.

Pure Presentation Architecture

Performs zero changes to your journal entries or database schema; simply enhances the dynamic report rendering layer.

User Workflow

How to Use Invoice Drill-down in 4 Steps

Seamlessly inspect product lines directly inside your financial reports.

Step 01
Open Partner Ledger

Navigate to Accounting → Reporting → Partner Ledger.

View: Enterprise Partner Ledger
Step 02
Unfold Partner Entity

Expand any customer or vendor to inspect their posted journal items.

Display: Invoice Rows with Arrows
Step 03
Click Invoice Arrow

Click the arrow icon next to any invoice number to expand its contents.

Trigger: Instant On-Demand Fetch
Step 04
Review Product Lines

View individual items, quantities, UoM, and subtotals right on screen.

Result: Complete Transaction Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this module work on Odoo Community Edition?

This specific drill-down module extends the dynamic interactive reporting engine of Odoo Enterprise (account_reports). It is designed exclusively for Enterprise editions.

Will this break when Odoo receives minor core updates?

No. The module utilizes Odoo's official public API methods (_get_generic_line_id) rather than custom string concatenation, ensuring seamless compatibility.

Does this affect report export to PDF or Excel?

The drill-down provides dynamic on-screen exploration. Standard report exports continue to generate cleanly as configured in your Odoo report settings.
